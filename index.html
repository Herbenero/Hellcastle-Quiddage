Pitch-to-board translation

Three top hoops become high-value targets, the center bull becomes the Quaffle core, and a moving Snitch bonus adds a dramatic finishing mechanic.

Designed like a wizard pub showdown.

This concept blends pub darts with the theatrical rhythm of Quidditch. Instead of copying the sport literally, it distills the fantasy into visible layers: hoops for goal scoring, rotating danger sectors for Bludgers, and one elusive Snitch event that can flip the result in an instant.

Hoop scoring Players aim for ring zones mapped to the three Quidditch hoops, each with escalating point values by distance and risk.
Bludger penalties Selected dark sectors act as hazards. Hit them and your current turn loses momentum or triggers a foul effect.
Beater defense Optional team mode allows one player to remove or redirect a penalty using a single defensive throw token.
Snitch finish A randomized moving target appears late-game. Striking it grants a major score burst and may immediately end the match.

How the magical darts system works.

Keep the structure familiar for pub play, but let the fiction create tension. Every round represents a possession on the pitch. Your darts become Quaffle attacks, tactical blocks, and one desperate Snitch chase.

Core rules

01
Open with the Quaffle

Each player throws three darts per turn. Standard outer board hits score base possession points, while designated hoop rings multiply them.

02
Hit the hoops

Small hoop, middle hoop, and wide hoop are worth different values. Consecutive hoop hits in one turn trigger a “passing play” combo bonus.

03
Avoid the Bludgers

Predetermined hazard wedges count as Bludger strikes. They can cancel your highest dart, subtract points, or force the next player into a risky throw.

04
Release the Snitch

Once a player crosses the score threshold, the Golden Snitch enters play. A successful hit awards 150 points and may end the match depending on mode.

Board map

Top hoops = goal multipliers
Dark wedges = Bludger hazards
Outer ring = possession points
Bull = Quaffle core

Game modes

House Duel

Classic 1v1. First to target score, unless the Snitch creates a comeback finish.

Three Chasers

Team mode where throws rotate and combo chains stack across players in the same house.

Pub Cup

Knockout bracket mode with shorter rounds, sudden-death Snitch triggers, and heavy crowd energy.

Scoring with drama, not just arithmetic.

The goal is to feel cinematic. Hoop lanes reward accuracy, central hits reward control, and the Snitch is a strategic pressure mechanic rather than a random gimmick. You can tune it for casual bars, fan conventions, or premium collector sets with illuminated boards.
