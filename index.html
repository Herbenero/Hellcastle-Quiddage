House rules

Gryffindor — Bold Retry

Once per match, upgrade one weak throw worth 10 or less into a 30-point strike.

Slytherin — Cunning Curse

Once per match, reduce the rival’s next shot above 30 points by 20.

Ravenclaw — Strategy Ring

Once per match, your next bull or center-hoop precision shot gains +15.

Hufflepuff — Steady Chain

Once per match, hitting three different scoring zones in one turn grants +25.

Moonstone — Lunar Aim

Once per match, call a target zone. If your next throw lands there, its score is doubled.

Emberwing — Flame Surge

Once per match, your next high-value hit empowers the following dart by +10. Emberwing also ignores one Bludger penalty each match.

Houses are balanced as tactical nudges rather than automatic wins. They amplify timing, pressure, and precision.

Quidditch Darts Rulebook

1. Basic Gameplay: Throw 3 physical darts per turn at your dartboard. Use the on-screen keypad to log each physical hit into the app.

2. Scoring Zones:
• Outer Ring: 10 pts
• Bullseye: 25 pts
• Lower Hoop: 30 pts
• Center Hoop: 50 pts
• High Hoop: 70 pts

3. Hazards (Bludgers): Landing on a Bludger wedge results in a -20 penalty unless mitigated by a house power or shield.

4. The Golden Snitch: When any team reaches 75% of target score, the Golden Snitch unlocks. Tap the Snitch button on the keypad or visual board when caught for 150 points and an instant victory.

5. House Powers: Each house has one unique, once-per-match ability available on your team card during your turn.
